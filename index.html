Who We Are

DRIVEN BY
PASSION,
BUILT IN MADRAS.

Madras Driven started as a small circle of car enthusiasts in Chennai who believed that automotive culture deserved its own stage in South India. What began as late-night meets on the ECR has grown into one of the city's most electrifying communities.

We organize events that bring together builders, drivers, and fans — from sprawling car shows and track days to curated night meets where the city's finest machines come alive under the lights.

This isn't just about cars. It's about the culture, the friendships, and the relentless love for machines that move us.
